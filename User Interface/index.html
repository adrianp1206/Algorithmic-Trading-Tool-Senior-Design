What we offer:

Our platform leverages advanced predictive machine learning models to generate trade recommendations across a variety of stocks while simultaneously simulating a trading environment to illustrate the daily performance of our models, ultimately empowering the average investor to make more informed decisions

Access the platform

Platform components

Fundamental Indicators

Indicators such as ROE, PE Ratio, ROIC, ... are fed into our models to increase model accuracy

Technical Indicators

Indicators such as Moving Averages, Sharpe Ratio, Bollinger Bands, are used to improve models accuracy

Long Short-Term Memory

Algorithmic model that analyzes historical price data and provides a next day predicted price

XGBoost

Analyzes a broad ranges of data to output a probability score measuring the chance of a stock going up or down

Natural Language Processing

Analyzes real-time news feeds and social medias to provide a numerical score describing market sentiment

Reinforcement Learning

Optimizes trading decisions by simulating trading scenarios outputting a trading action that maximizes portfolio returns
